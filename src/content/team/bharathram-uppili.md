**Computational Biology** — Ph.D., Academy of Scientific and Innovative Research / CSIR–Institute of Genomics and Integrative Biology (2025)
**Bioinformatics** — B.Tech, SASTRA University (2016)

Bharathram completed his Ph.D. under the mentorship of Dr. Mohammed Faruq at CSIR-IGIB, where he designed and built STRIDE-DB, a public database cataloging short tandem repeat instability across global and Indian populations, and used it to identify disease-associated repeat loci in neurodegenerative disorders. He also contributed to GenomeIndia, managing end-to-end variant calling and quality control for over 2,500 genomes, and worked on rare-disease exome genomics and SARS-CoV-2 genomic surveillance in Indian cohorts.

His current research focuses on integrating public multi-omics data to identify genes associated with age-related macular degeneration, including a cross-dataset single-cell pipeline resolving myeloid substates across independent retina and RPE-choroid scRNA-/snRNA-seq datasets. He works fluently across R, Python, and Bash-based genomics pipelines, applying statistical and machine-learning methods to variant and expression analysis at population scale.
