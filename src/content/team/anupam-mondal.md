Dr. Mondal is a computational biologist who began his career as a biotechnology engineer. During his graduate work, he studied genomic determinants underlying human–microbe interactions, before turning to the retina as a postdoctoral fellow at the NIH, where he explored the molecular mechanisms of retinal disease. His research now focuses on uncovering time-specific molecular patterns in vision disorders and aging through computational approaches, at UT Southwestern's Department of Ophthalmology.

Outside the lab, he enjoys climbing, running, mountain activities, history, travel, and cooking.
